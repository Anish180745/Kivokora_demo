Frequently Asked Questions

What does "trust before payments" actually mean?

It means we show you real work — strategy documents, designs, drafts — before asking for the next payment milestone, instead of asking you to pay upfront and hope for the best.

How is Kivokora different from hiring separate freelancers or agencies?

One team, one strategy, one point of contact. Instead of a logo designer, a website freelancer and an ads agency all working with different goals, everything at Kivokora is planned and executed together — so nothing falls through the cracks.

What happens if I'm not happy with the work?

Because delivery is milestone-based, you review and approve work as it's produced — so issues get caught and fixed early, not after everything is already live.

Do you only work with large businesses?

No — we work with growing businesses of every size across e-commerce, local services, D2C brands and B2B companies. The free Growth Audit is how we figure out what makes sense for your stage.

What's the catch with the free Growth Audit?

There isn't one. It's a genuine look at your current marketing, website and brand with real recommendations — whether or not you decide to work with us afterward.
