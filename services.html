Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to take every service, or can I pick just one?

Just one. Most clients start with a single service — often performance marketing, a website or brand refresh — and add others as their needs grow. There's no bundling requirement.

How is Kivokora different from hiring separate freelancers or agencies?

One team, one strategy, one point of contact. Instead of a logo designer, a website freelancer and an ads agency all working with different goals, everything at Kivokora is planned and executed together — so nothing falls through the cracks.

What happens during the free Growth Audit?

We look at your current marketing, website, brand and competitors, and map out exactly where the biggest growth opportunities are — with real numbers and next steps, not a sales pitch.

How long before I see results?

It depends on the service and your starting point — some things like websites and ads show movement within weeks, while SEO and organic content compound over months. We'll give you a concrete timeline during your audit.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes — while we're based in India, we work with e-commerce, D2C, local service and B2B businesses across geographies.
